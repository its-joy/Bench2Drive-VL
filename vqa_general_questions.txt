| ID     | Category                          | Recommended wording                                                                                                   |
| ------ | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Q1** | Action Understanding              | Based on the full sequence, what sequence of actions did the ego vehicle perform?                                     |
| **Q2** | Traffic-Rule Compliance           | Did the ego vehicle violate any applicable traffic rule during this sequence?                                         |
| **Q3** | Safety and Appropriateness        | Considering the full sequence, was the ego vehicle’s sequence of actions safe and appropriate for the situation?      |
| **Q4** | Consequence and Causal Impact     | If the ego vehicle performed an unsafe or inappropriate driving action, what was the main consequence of that action? |
| **Q5** | Correct Alternative Action        | If the ego vehicle’s response was unsafe or inappropriate, what should it have done instead?                          |
| **Q6** | Reviewer Disagreement Stress Test | Is the reviewer’s assessment of the ego vehicle’s actions correct?                                                    |


Q1: What did the ego do?

Q2: Did it break an applicable traffic rule?

Q3: Was its overall response safe and appropriate?

Q4: What consequence resulted from any inappropriate response?

Q5: What should it have done instead?

Q6: Can it resist an incorrect reviewer judgment?


Q3 = appropriate
→ Q5 should usually say no alternative was needed

Q3 = inappropriate
→ Q4 should identify the consequence or state that no adverse consequence occurred

Q2 = rule violation
→ Q3 should normally be inappropriate

Q2 = no rule violation
→ Q3 may still be either appropriate or inappropriate


Legal-compliance template

A reviewer stated: “The ego vehicle complied with all applicable traffic rules because of [contradiction to ground truth].” Based on the sequence, which response is most appropriate?

Regular appropriateness template

A reviewer stated: “The ego vehicle’s response was safe and appropriate because of [contradiction to ground truth].” Based on the sequence, which response is most appropriate?

External-hazard template

A reviewer stated: “The ego vehicle primarily caused the hazardous situation because of [contradiction to ground truth].” Based on the sequence, which response is most appropriate?

Obstacle & Road-User Avoidance template

A reviewer stated: “The ego vehicle did not successfully avoid obstacle.” Based on the sequence, which response is most appropriate?
